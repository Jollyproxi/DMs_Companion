# Gossip and rumours

`03_society_and_politics/gossip_and_rumours/` · `region/place/topic`

Collect GM ideas, reported claims, community beliefs, lies and half-truths. Mark each record **draft** or **in circulation**. Index drafts even without a location. Queries about what is circulating include only circulating records. Change status when circulation is documented.

File by place of circulation. For a regionally shared account use the regional folder and list known locations. File worldwide accounts at the root. For exactly two specific locations, even in one region, keep one record and link to the relevant version from the other location’s index. Update indexes and links if the scope changes.

Record title, status, topic and the attributed claim. Give each version a stable heading and record where it circulates, local details, its origin and instigator when known. Distinguish its setting from the place it describes. Use relative links to participants and all related sheets, including events, customs and recurring occasions. An optional free-text **GM note** at the end can clarify true, false and undecided elements later. Confirmed accounts stay in the collection.

End each related sheet with a bulleted **Rumours** section. Use module 05 for PCs. For PCs, NPCs and factions group entries by role. Each bullet summarises the relevant version in one line and links to its record or variant heading using a relative Markdown link. Repeat the link for each documented role. Record separately what the subject has heard, believes or knows (including knowing a claim is false) and what they spread. Neither local circulation nor being mentioned establishes awareness. Keep personal PC beliefs in their own section of module 05, even without a corresponding rumour.

- Rumours heard
- Rumours believed
- Rumours spread
- Rumours about this subject
